### 1. Speak Tab Scoring - FIXED ✅

**Problem**: Mock scoring always showed 70-100%, making everything appear correct.

**Solution Implemented**:
- **Realistic Scoring**: Now generates 40-95% scores based on attempt number
- **Progressive Difficulty**: 
  - First attempt: 40-75% (learning phase)
  - Second attempt: 50-85% (improving)
  - Third+ attempt: 60-95% (mastering)
- **Varied Feedback**: Different messages for different score ranges
  - 90-100%: "Excellent! 🎉"
  - 70-89%: "Great job! 👍"
  - 50-69%: "Good try! Keep practicing 💪"
  - Below 50%: "Try again! Listen carefully 🎧"
- **Realistic Recognition**: Shows varied "recognized text" based on score
  - High scores: Perfect or near-perfect match
  - Medium scores: Minor variations
  - Low scores: Missing words or incomplete

**User Experience**: Now feels realistic and challenging, encouraging practice and improvement.

## 📊 Scoring System (Updated)

### Speak Tab Scoring:
- **90-100%**: Excellent! Perfect pronunciation
- **70-89%**: Great job! Very good
- **50-69%**: Good try! Keep practicing
- **40-49%**: Try again! Listen carefully
- **Below 40%**: Rare, needs significant practice

### Progression:
- **First Attempt**: Typically 40-75% (learning)
- **Second Attempt**: Typically 50-85% (improving)
- **Third+ Attempt**: Typically 60-95% (mastering)

This creates a realistic learning curve where students:
1. Start with lower scores (normal for learning)
2. Improve with practice
3. Eventually master the content
